Game of Emergence uses the following third-party material.

The hat tiling's substitution rules in engine/src/hat.rs are derived from
hatviz by Craig S. Kaplan, https://github.com/isohedral/hatviz

The tiling itself is described in "An aperiodic monotile" by David Smith,
Joseph Samuel Myers, Craig S. Kaplan and Chaim Goodman-Strauss, 2023.

Hat glider A (its rule table and seed) is by James Edmond, from monotile-ca,
https://github.com/jamesedmond/monotile-ca, described in "Gliders on Aperiodic
Monotilings: Cellular Automata on the Hat and Spectre", arXiv:2609.22579. It is
used under the MIT license, one of the two licenses offered there.
